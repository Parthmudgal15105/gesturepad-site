Setup, pairing and everyday questions for the Mac app and its iPhone / iPad companion.

Release in preparation. GesturePad is not yet available on the App Store. These instructions describe the current app and may be refined before release.

Start with both apps

You need a Mac running macOS 14 or later and an iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Connect them to the same Wi-Fi network and keep GesturePad open on the Mac.

Pair your iPhone or iPad

  1. On the Mac, open GesturePad, select Devices, then choose Pair New Device….
  2. On your iPhone or iPad, choose Pair with Your Mac and allow Local Network access.
  3. Select your Mac under Macs on This Network. Enter its 12-character pairing code, or choose Scan QR Code when available.
  4. Choose Pair and approve the device on your Mac. Pairing codes work once and expire after five minutes.
  5. Use Practice Mode to get familiar with gestures. Return to the connected canvas to send actions to the Mac.

Common questions

My Mac does not appear. What should I check?

Make sure GesturePad is running on the Mac and both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network. Check Local Network permission in system settings on each device where offered. Guest Wi-Fi or network isolation can prevent devices from discovering one another. Bring the companion to the foreground and try again.

Do I need to pair again after a disconnect?

A disconnected device can stay paired. First reopen GesturePad on both devices and check the network. If the companion is offline, open its Settings and choose Reconnect Now. Pair again only if you intentionally forgot the Mac or removed the device, or the stored pairing is no longer usable.

A gesture is recognized, but the Mac action does not work.

Check the active profile, its mapping and whether GesturePad is paused. Keyboard and media actions require permission for GesturePad in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility on the Mac. The intended app should be in front for keyboard actions, and any app or named Shortcut used by a mapping must be installed. Practice deliberately runs no Mac actions.

Where do I edit mappings and workflows?

Use GesturePad on the Mac to configure gesture actions, app-specific profiles, the Everywhere fallback and multi-step workflows. The companion is a gesture canvas. Its reference shows mappings for the current profile; a manually locked profile stays active until you unlock it.

How can I practice without triggering anything?

Open Practice Mode from Settings on the companion. Practice recognizes gestures without sending Mac actions. When connected, it uses your active mappings; offline, it provides examples.

How do I pause control quickly?

Use the companion’s Pause control, or hold four fingers for 1.5 seconds to pause or resume. On iPad, system multitasking gestures may intercept some four- and five-finger input; use the visible Pause control if needed.

Is camera access required?

Camera access is used for scanning the Mac’s pairing QR code. You can enter the pairing code manually instead. The camera is not used to recognize your gestures.

How do I remove a pairing?

On the companion, open Settings and choose Forget This Mac. Also remove that device from the Mac app’s Devices screen. Removing a device on the Mac revokes its authorization; a new pairing is needed before it can control the Mac again.
